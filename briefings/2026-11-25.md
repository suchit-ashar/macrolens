## India ↔ Global Linkages

1. Fed-hike pricing → higher US short-rate expectations and a firmer dollar → potential pressure on the rupee, foreign flows and equity valuation multiples.
2. Hormuz shipping risk → higher freight, insurance and crude costs → pressure on India's import bill, inflation and current account; lower crude would reverse part of this channel.
3. USTR Section 301 findings → possible additional duties on India and 15 other economies → uncertainty for steel, autos, chemicals, electronics, solar and other export-linked manufacturers. India is explicitly among the 16 economies under investigation; tariff rates are not yet confirmed. [USTR, 11 Mar](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/march/ustr-initiates-section-301-investigations-relating-structural-excess-capacity-and-production) · [Reuters, 24–25 Nov](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-administration-set-unveil-new-tariffs-after-probe-on-excess-factory-2026-11-24/)
4. Global semiconductor selling → weaker technology risk appetite → pressure on regional chip markets, although India's IT index rose 1.69% on 23 November and the sector gained 2% at today's open.

These are transmission channels, not proof that one event caused every market move.
